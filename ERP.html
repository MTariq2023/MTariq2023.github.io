ERP Business Analytics Demo

Odoo ERP · PostgreSQL · Power BI

Overview

This demo project showcases how I set up a sample ERP system (Odoo), generated sales and purchase transactions, extracted data from the backend PostgreSQL database using SQL, and then visualized business KPIs in Power BI. The project highlights end-to-end ERP data integration, transformation, and analytics.

Walkthrough

1. Odoo ERP dashboard after installation and module configuration.
2. Created sample customers and vendors in ERP to simulate business transactions.
3. Configured demo employees and departments for access/security setup.
4. Added demo products with categories for sales and purchases.
5. Captured customer sales orders with multiple products.
6. Logged vendor purchases to simulate supply chain activity.
7. Tracked order processing through ERP workflow states.
8. Generated invoices for recorded sales transactions.
9. Ran ERP sales analysis reports to validate orders and revenue trends.
10. Managed ERP users and roles for security and module demo.
11. Extracted raw customer data from PostgreSQL using SQL queries.
12. Cleaned and joined sales orders with customers for analysis.
13. Verified purchase order data structured and ready for Power BI import.
14. Final Power BI dashboard showing total sales, purchases, stock, and profit trends.
